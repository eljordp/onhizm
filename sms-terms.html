SMS Terms

How the optional ONHIZM text program works. Updated November 1, 2026.

The ONHIZM Text Program

If you choose to enroll, ONHIZM may send recurring automated marketing texts to the phone number you provide, including product drops, offers, and promotions. Message frequency varies.

SMS signup is optional. Consent is not a condition of purchase. An SMS signup offer applies only as described where the offer is displayed. Joining SMS does not also subscribe you to email marketing.

How You Join

Enter your phone number in the ONHIZM SMS signup form, select the separate SMS consent checkbox, and submit the form. The checkbox is unchecked by default. The form records the consent wording, date and time, and technical signup information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Message & Data Charges

Your mobile carrier's message and data rates may apply to texts you send or receive. Check your mobile plan for details.

Stop Texts or Get Help

Reply STOP to an ONHIZM text to unsubscribe from the SMS program. Reply HELP for help, or email onhizm@gmail.com about the program or your subscription.

Unsubscribing from marketing texts does not cancel an order. For order questions, contact us using the same email address.

SMS Data

ONHIZM does not sell mobile numbers or SMS consent records, or share them with third parties for those parties' own marketing. Service providers may process this information to operate ONHIZM's text program.

Privacy & Purchases

Our Privacy Policy explains the signup information collected and the services supporting the store. Our Purchase Policy addresses orders. These SMS Terms describe the optional text program separately.
