Privacy Policy

Information handled by our store and optional text program. Updated November 1, 2026.

Information You Provide

When you place an order, Shopify handles the checkout information needed for your purchase, such as your contact details, delivery address, and payment information. ONHIZM uses order information to handle purchases and respond to order questions.

If you contact ONHIZM, we receive the contact details and message you send. If you choose to join our SMS program, we collect your phone number and record your marketing consent.

SMS Signup Records

Our text signup records include the phone number, consent choice, consent wording, signup date and time, the page used to sign up, the intended destination page, IP address, browser or device information, and the associated discount offer. The signup sends these records to Omnisend to create or update the SMS contact.

We use this information to manage the text program, record consent, apply the signup offer, and handle subscription questions. Joining SMS does not also subscribe you to email marketing.

Cookies & Browser Storage

The storefront uses browser storage to remember the Shopify cart, an SMS discount or access marker, recent cart activity, and whether the signup prompt was dismissed. The saved signup marker contains the signup time, the last four digits of the phone number, and the discount code.

The site also loads Omnisend's tracking script and sends page-view events. Omnisend may use cookies or similar browser identifiers to support its tracking and marketing tools.

You can manage cookies and clear site storage in your browser settings. Doing so can reset your cart, discount marker, or signup preferences on that device.

Services Supporting the Store

Shopify supports product and cart functions and handles checkout. Omnisend processes SMS signup records and supports marketing and site tracking. Information is also processed by the providers needed to host the site and deliver messages.

SMS Data

ONHIZM does not sell mobile numbers or SMS consent records, or share them with third parties for those parties' own marketing. Service providers may process this information to operate ONHIZM's text program.

Your Text Preferences

SMS signup is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to leave the text program, or HELP for assistance. You can also contact us with questions about your information or subscription.

See our SMS Terms for program details and our Purchase Policy for order policies.
